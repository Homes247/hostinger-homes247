Our Story Begins Here

At Homes247, we believe everyone deserves their dream home and our portal will be your guide.

Real estate is one of life's biggest decisions, and finding the right property deserves more than endless scrolling. Since 2017, Homes247 has brought together over 30 lakh property listings on a single real estate portal, making it easier to discover apartments, villas, plots, commercial spaces and rental properties across India's growing markets.

So we built Homes247.in as one place that stays with you through all of it. Not just search, but the site visit, the home loan support, right up to getting your keys

We're also one of the trusted real estate portals for renting, reselling, and researching property in India, and it's completely free to post if you're an owner or agent.

Whether you're buying your first home, renting your next one, or listing a property to sell, Homes247 is built to go the distance with you.

Founder & CEO, Homes247

An alumni of IIM Ahmedabad and engineer by trade, he fell in love with sales and marketing from a very early age. Today, that same instinct shapes every decision at Homes247, build what the industry is missing, a vision that guides us forward even today.
